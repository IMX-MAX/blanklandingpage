what if music was yours

[blank] is a local Android music player for people who like to be free. Your library lives on your device. No accounts required, no ads, no noise.

Download APK View source

Yours, locally

The songs you already own, played straight from your device. Nothing tracked, nothing sold, nothing in between you and the record.

Offline, entirely

Long-press a song or album and make it available offline. Audio, artwork and lyrics are stored on the device — it plays with no connection at all.

Lyrics, in time

Synced lyrics from the AMLL TTML database and LRCLIB. Word-perfect when the timing exists, plain text when it doesn't. Nothing invented.

Free, as in freedom

Open source on GitHub. Build it yourself, change what you like, keep what matters. A quiet player for a loud world.
