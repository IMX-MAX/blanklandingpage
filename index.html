the library

Everything you own,
one quiet grid.

Open the app and you're already home. A grid of covers, your records, nothing else — no tabs to hunt through, no noise in the way.

the player

Play it your way

A pill-shaped bar with a live waveform rides along the bottom wherever you go. Skip, scrub, or sit with a song — the queue is yours to arrange.

the lyrics

Words that keep time

Synced lyrics from the AMLL TTML database and LRCLIB. Word-perfect highlights when timing exists, plain text when it doesn't — nothing invented.

the freedom

Take it offline

Long-press a song or album and make it available offline. Audio, artwork and lyrics are stored on the device — downloaded songs play with no connection at all.
