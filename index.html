what if music was yours

[blank] is a local Android music player for people who like to be free. Your library lives on your device. No accounts, no ads, no noise.

Your library, at a glance

Everything you own, laid out as a quiet grid of covers. Open the app and you're already home — no tabs to hunt through, nothing between you and your records.

Dig into an album

Tap a cover and the album unfolds — artwork, tracks, and everything you'd want to play. One screen, one record, no distractions.

Play it your way

A pill-shaped bar with a live waveform rides along the bottom wherever you go. Skip, scrub, or sit with a song — the queue is yours to arrange.

Lyrics that keep time

Synced lyrics come from the AMLL TTML database and LRCLIB. Word-perfect highlights when timing exists, plain text when it doesn't — nothing invented.

Take it offline

Long-press a song or album and make it available offline. Audio, artwork and lyrics are stored on the device, and downloaded songs play with no connection at all.
